Story mode

From Recruit to BABY BOSS

Five chapters and sixteen story missions, each with an objective, a waypoint and three stars to earn. Climb the ranks: Recruit, Runner, Enforcer, Lieutenant, Underboss and finally BABY BOSS.

I

The New Kid

Rank: Recruit. Drop-Off, Juice Run, Wheels. Learn the ropes, sneak a cookie past the nanny, smuggle juice boxes to the ball pit and race Knuckles on a ride-on toy car.

II

Nap Time Heist

Rank: Runner. Blanket Bandit, Cookie Jar Job, Turf. Swipe nap blankets while the nanny makes her rounds, pull off the heist of the cookie jar, and win back the sandbox with your first toy water pistol.

III

Sandbox Wars

Rank: Enforcer. Tricycle Chase, The Crayon Racket, Ball Pit Showdown. Chase down a courier on a trike, collect the crayons, and hold the neon ball pit against the Crib Crew.

IV

The Rat

Rank: Lieutenant. Squealer, Janitor’s Closet, Parking Lot Getaway. Someone is telling the nannies everything. Tail the suspect, grab the master key, and escape at five stars.

V

Naptime Is Over

Rank: Underboss. Truce, Lockdown, The Office, The Big Chair. Team up with your old rivals, spring your crew from the time-out corner and storm the Director’s office.

Twelve Golden Pacifiers are hidden around the daycare. Find them all.

Heat

Five pacifier stars

Get spotted and your heat rises. The higher it goes, the more grown-ups come looking.

1–2 stars

Nannies search and give chase. Get scooped up and it’s the time-out corner.

3–4 stars

The chase gets faster and the search circle gets wider. Much harder to shake.

5 stars

Sirens glow at the edges of the screen and Director Grimsby herself comes out of her office. Run for the getaway car.

Lose the heat

Break line of sight, duck into a hiding spot, or floor it out of the search circle on a ride-on. A BONK makes a grown-up stumble for a moment, but it raises your heat.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really offline?

Yes. The whole story plays on your phone with no account and no internet connection needed. Ads and the optional purchase need a connection when they appear, but the game itself does not.

Is it free?

Daycare Dons is free to download. It shows ads from Google AdMob and has two optional extras: Remove Ads (a one-time purchase) and Daycare VIP (a subscription: no ads, a daily cookie gift, double mission cookies and a golden onesie). The whole story can be finished without either.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. The whole story is free to finish. Cookies and rep are earned by playing, and nothing you can buy makes the nannies easier or the heat lower.

Where do ads show up?

One banner on the title screen, never during play. A full-screen ad can appear between missions, never in your first two missions, at most every other mission and at least three minutes apart, and never during play or a story scene. Rewarded ads are always your choice: watch one to double the cookies from a mission. Remove Ads turns off the banner and the between-mission ads.

Is it for kids?

No. It is a cartoon comedy for teens and grown-ups who remember nap time, with mild cartoon mischief: toy water pistols, splashes and time-outs. Nobody gets hurt. It is not for children under 13.

What phones does it run on?

Android phones. Low, Medium and High graphics settings let it run smoothly on older phones and look its best on newer ones.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud save. NEW GAME on the title screen starts over, and uninstalling deletes everything. The Remove Ads purchase and the Daycare VIP subscription are tied to your Google Play account and can be restored.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Open an issue on our GitHub page. There are templates for bug reports, ideas and privacy questions.
